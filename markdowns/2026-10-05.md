# 📚 DriftTTS: Few-Step Text-to-Speech Without Distillation via Distribution-Matching Drift

🚀 URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03390

## 🌏 Abstract (원문)
Few-step neural text-to-speech models often rely on shortened diffusion or flow-matching schedules, or on distillation from pretrained multi-step teachers. To avoid these dependencies, we present DriftTTS, a few-step mel-spectrogram generator trained without a generative teacher, distillation, or adversarial discrimination. DriftTTS uses a distribution-matching drift objective in a mel-domain feature space defined by raw mels and a frozen masked-autoencoder encoder pretrained on the same LJSpeech training split. On-policy rollout trains the decoder on its own intermediate states and supports inference up to the trained rollout depth. On LJSpeech, DriftTTS at NFE=4 achieves 3.87	dB MCD and 3.7% WER, compared with 3.85	dB and 3.4% for Matcha-TTS. In a fully paired blind listening test, DriftTTS obtains 4.18 MOS, compared with 3.96 for Matcha-TTS and 4.22 for ground truth. These results demonstrate competitive few-step synthesis without a pretrained generative teacher. Code can be found athttps://github.com/BASHLab/driftTTS.git

# 📚 Learning When to Commit from Partial Speech for End-to-End Simultaneous Speech Translation

🚀 URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02612

## 🌏 Abstract (원문)
Simultaneous speech translation must emit useful target text before the source is complete while preserving every committed token. We adapt a full-utterance speech language model using prefix supervision derived from its own complete- and partial-waveform translations, requiring neither transcripts nor human translations. We compare single-turn forced-prefix and multi-turn append-only decoding, use a confidence threshold to control the inference-time quality–latency trade-off, and vary the density of training prefixes with a separate synthesis margin. On FLEURS and CoVoST	2 in three language directions, prefix training improves quality–latency frontiers over the unadapted model, and confidence provides the broadest consistently competitive operating range. Multi-turn decoding is generally stronger at low latency; under multi-turn training, commit-calibration error falls by 63–68% overall and 68–80% at early prefixes, whereas single-turn training provides only modest overall calibration gains and no early-prefix improvement. A small synthesis margin sometimes extends the frontier to lower latency, particularly on shorter utterances, while a larger margin degrades translation quality and calibration. Prefix adaptation therefore improves simultaneous speech translation, especially under multi-turn append-only decoding, while synthesis density introduces a non-monotonic quality–latency trade-off.

# 📚 MintFlow: Minimal Trajectory Intervention for Constrained Flow Matching

🚀 URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.02260

## 🌏 Abstract (원문)
Flow matching models excel at generative modeling, and many downstream applications require their samples to satisfy prescribed constraints, such as observed measurements and physical laws. However, existing constrained samplers often face a trade-off:enforcing constraints can substantially displace samples from the pretrained data distribution. To address this trade-off, we introduceMintFlow, a training-free constrained sampling framework that formulates constraint enforcement as a minimal intervention on the pretrained flow trajectory. MintFlow seeks the minimal perturbation of an intermediate flow state such that its subsequent evolution under the pretrained flow field satisfies the target constraint.By minimally perturbing the flow state while keeping the pretrained flow field unchanged, MintFlow enforces the constraint while minimizing unnecessary deviation from the pretrained distribution. An adjoint formulation yields a closed-form expression for this perturbation, eliminating expensive iterative optimization. Furthermore, MintFlow adaptively selects the intervention time to balance the required perturbation magnitude with its amplification by the remaining flow.Across a range of tasks in generative vision and physical system modeling, MintFlow achieves competitive constraint satisfaction while preserving the pretrained generative distribution substantially better than state-of-the-art constrained methods.

## 🔍 Methods & Results
- MintFlow는 사후 투영이나 사전 학습된 플로우 수정 없이, 생성된 궤적에 대한 최소한의 개입을 통해 제약 조건을 강제합니다.
- 중간 플로우 상태 `u_s`에 최소 섭동 `δ`를 가하여, 이 섭동된 상태가 사전 학습된 플로우 필드에 의해 전파될 때 최종 제약 조건 `H(Φ(T, s, u_s + δ)) = 0`을 만족하도록 합니다.
- 이 최소 섭동 문제는 플로우 맵의 수반 민감도(adjoint sensitivity)를 사용하여 최소-노름 섭동(minimum-norm perturbation)을 계산함으로써 해결되며, 이는 선형화된 최종 제약 조건에 대한 Moore–Penrose 유사 역행렬을 통해 닫힌 형식으로 표현됩니다.
- 최종 상태의 중간 상태에 대한 민감도(Jacobian `A_s`)는 수반 방정식을 통해 효율적으로 계산되어 고차원 시스템에서도 명시적인 전체 Jacobian 계산 없이 적용 가능합니다.
- MintFlow는 필요한 섭동 크기와 나머지 플로우에 의한 섭동 증폭 사이의 균형을 맞추는 기준 `J(s)`를 최소화하여 개입 시간 `s`를 적응적으로 선택합니다.
- 제안된 선형화된 보정은 제약 조건 만족도(2차 최종 제약 조건 잔차)와 분포 보존(원래 플로우 매칭 분포로부터의 편차 제한)에 대한 이론적 보장을 제공합니다.
- MintFlow는 생성 비전 및 물리 시스템 모델링 작업에서 경쟁력 있는 제약 조건 만족도를 달성하며, 최첨단 방법보다 사전 학습된 생성 분포를 훨씬 더 잘 보존합니다.
- 제약 조건 잔차가 허용 오차를 초과할 경우, 보정된 궤적에 대해 반복적으로 알고리즘을 적용하여 잔차를 줄이는 반복 정제 과정을 사용합니다.

# 📚 Getting Your Guidance Weights Right in diffusion and flow-matching posterior sampling

🚀 URL: https://arxiv.org/html/2610.03503

## 🌏 Abstract (원문)
Training-free posterior sampling methods, also known as Plug-and-Play methods, leverage pretrained unconditional diffusion or flow-matching models to solve inverse problems. Most existing approaches rely on guidance weights to balance, at each time step, prior information from the unconditional score or velocity network with measurement consistency, yet the tuning of these weights is often not discussed and is largely left to heuristics. We introduce a simple and principled offline strategy for automatically tuning these guidance weights. Our key observation is that, at each time step, the conditional denoising score-matching objective for diffusion models, or the conditional flow-matching objective for flow-matching models, is a least-squares objective. Therefore, when the conditional prediction is expressed as a weighted sum of the unconditional network output and a measurement-guidance term, optimizing over these weights reduces to a two-dimensional linear least-squares problem. The resulting time-dependent guidance weights can be optimized offline for a given measurement operator, noise level and sampler at the cost of a single minibatch of sampling trajectories, without retraining or fine-tuning the pretrained generative model. Instantiated with the standard Tweedie-based measurement-consistency term, our approach improves posterior sampling and achieves state-of-the-art reconstruction performance across diffusion- and flow-matching-based methods. Moreover, the optimized guidance weights enable diffusion samplers to reduce the number of sampling steps from 1000 to 50 with no significant degradation in reconstruction quality. Code will be made available.

## 🔍 Methods & Results
- Plug-and-Play 방법에서 시간 의존적 가이던스 가중치를 자동적이고 효율적으로 보정하는 것을 목표로 합니다.
- 핵심 관찰은 조건부 노이즈 제거 스코어 매칭 또는 조건부 플로우 매칭 목표가 가이던스 가중치에 대한 선형 최소 제곱 문제로 표현될 수 있다는 것입니다.
- 초기 '시뮬레이션 없는 보정' 전략은 각 시간 단계에서 가중치를 독립적으로 최적화하며, 단일 미니배치를 사용하여 효율적입니다.
- 시뮬레이션 없는 보정의 한계는 제한된 매개변수와 비최적 항으로 인해 실제 사후 궤적에 근접함을 보장하지 못한다는 것입니다.
- 이를 해결하기 위해 '시뮬레이션 기반 보정(SIMCA)' 전략을 제안합니다. 이는 전방 프로세스 밀도를 근사 사후 분포로 대체하고, 현재 가중치만 최적화하는 탐욕적(greedy) 접근 방식을 사용합니다.
- SIMCA는 단일 미니배치 샘플링 궤적만 필요하므로 실용적이며, 보정된 가중치는 추론 시 오버헤드를 발생시키지 않습니다.
- 제안된 방법은 사전 학습된 생성 모델의 재학습, 아키텍처 변경 또는 샘플러 수정 없이 적용 가능합니다.
- SIMCA는 사후 샘플링을 개선하고 확산 및 플로우 매칭 기반 방법에서 최첨단 재구성 성능을 달성합니다.
- 최적화된 가이던스 가중치를 통해 확산 샘플러는 샘플링 단계를 1000에서 50으로 줄여도 재구성 품질에 큰 저하가 없음을 보여주었습니다.
